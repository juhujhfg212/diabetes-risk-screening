Diabetes Risk Screening

A quick, offline screening tool based on CDC health survey data.

⚠️ Important — Please Read

This is NOT a medical diagnosis.

This tool estimates risk using a statistical model trained on survey data. It cannot detect, confirm, or rule out diabetes. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for real medical advice and testing.

How this works

  1. You'll answer {{ total_steps }} short steps of questions (about 2–3 minutes).
  2. Every field has a short explanation if you're unsure.
  3. The model estimates your risk from your answers.
  4. You'll see a risk band with tailored advice.

No data is sent anywhere. Everything runs on your own computer.
